# Evaluation: gates, probes and the selection set

Rules to respect when running or changing `evaluate-effect-model`, a gate, the knowledge or embedding probes, the control score or the selection set. This file does not describe the reports:

- Flags and what is printed: `python -m effects evaluate-effect-model --help`, `application/breakdowns.py`, `application/control_score.py`.
- What is measured: `specs/2026-10-30-ability-effect-model-gen3.md` § 14, `specs/024-ability-effect-model-gen2/contracts/knowledge-probes.md`.
- The selection-set file: `specs/025-ability-effect-model-gen3/contracts/selection-set.md`.
- Why: `experiments/2026-09-19-effect-knowledge-probes-design.md`, `experiments/2026-10-30-effect-model-gen3-design.md`.

## `evaluate-effect-model`

- Splits are never a flag. The held-out card list and both `game_id` sets come from `--checkpoint`: the corpus is append-only, and a recomputed split would score the gates partly on games the model trained on.
- `--corpus` defaults to the dataset the checkpoint recorded. A checkpoint recording none is refused, and so is a corpus whose manifest digest has changed. A `--variant-checkpoint` recording a different split or vocabulary hashes fails fast.
- It exits 0 after any gate verdict, printing `GATES FAILED: …`, and 1 on a corpus not wholly gen-3 or a checkpoint of another input layout.
- The three FR-116 checks never built (matched real-vs-fork agreement, the probe-diff re-check, the role-polarity probe) report `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`, printed `[todo]`, on every run. They are not skips: the corpus holds the records they would read.
- Every geometry check keys the cache on the checkpoint's encoding surface, so on a script checkpoint two lines sharing prose but not script stay two points.
- A line query that resolves to nothing is named in the report as unresolved, never dropped.
- Every breakdown and both gates read each record once, at no class weight. A validation stratum holds a record's row once plus once for every added copy, so a reader of distinct records dedupes by `record_id`.
- The selection set's reporting half is scored once, through the control score's own scorer. The selection halves belong to the trainer.

## The gates

- Gate 1, the identity baseline, asks whether the encoder is reading text. Pass/fail.
  - It is scored on the card-disjoint split's unique-text stratum: resolution records whose acting text no record of the curated `training/` stratum acts with. That is the only slice where a baseline holding a free vector per text cannot simply recall the answer.
  - Three margins must all hold: at least 0.05 absolute affected-gate F1 gain, at least 0.05 absolute zone-outcome accuracy gain, at least 5% relative negative-binomial deviance reduction, each model's deviance read under its own learned dispersions.
  - Zone accuracy is class-balanced over the observed outcomes, so answering "stayed" every time scores no better than chance.
  - A margin that cannot be measured fails.
  - There are three because they fail differently: the F1 says whether the model knows that something happens, the zone accuracy what, the deviance how much.
- Gate 2, the damage-step keyword canary, only routes: its per-keyword verdict decides whether the probe machinery is worth building.
  - Per keyword: at least 200 qualifying records and at least 70% direction agreement under a model-side perturbation, the carrier stripped of the keyword (its kept flag cleared and every line of the keyword dropped, printed or granted).
  - A slot holds every keyword of the lines sharing its key (a static granting two keywords: `SlotLine.keywords`). A strip drops the slot only when every keyword it holds is stripped; otherwise the slot stays, its text still stating the keyword, and the surface lists the carrier in `EffectHeadInput.unremovable`. Gate 2 leaves such a carrier unqualified: the observation is not scored, and the keyword's verdict counts it (`KeywordVerdict.unqualified`, a tail of its reason). A granted keyword with no line of its own (a printed conditional grant such as "max speed") is not detected.
  - The population is the real combats of the game-disjoint games. A probe fork or an intervention qualifies nothing.
  - A combat whose own changes end the game qualifies nothing (`sba_board.game_ends`, the test `sba_zone_move` reads): Forge's state-based check returns at the game's end before it destroys any creature, so such a combat shows no death the table expects. `qualifying_observations` leaves it out, so the survey's keyword threshold and `keyword_combat` do too, and the combat grids pair none of its creatures (`grid_edits.grid_block`).
  - A row qualifies a carrier only on damage dealt or taken in the record's own step (rule 510.4), and a phased-out creature or a last-known copy takes no part. The full qualification rules are in `domain/damage_step_keywords.py`, which the survey's keyword threshold and the `keyword_combat` detector read too.
  - The 200 counts distinct `record_id`s; agreement averages over (record, carrier) observations.
  - Fields are read through `HeadReading`, the gate's probability times the expected value, since a field head trains only where an entity was affected. A signed delta's expected value is its magnitude column (the mean absolute value over every scored row, zeros included) times `(P(+) − P(−)) / (P(+) + P(−))`, the sign's lean among the non-zero outcomes; the zero class is discounted once, by the magnitude.
  - Under-sampled routes the same way as wrong. Treating untested as passed is how a canary stops being one.
- Gate 3, the collapse canaries, is pass/fail: mean pairwise cosine at most 0.5 over 10,000 random pairs, top principal component at most 30% of variance.
  - One vector per unique ability text, unique by the text on the checkpoint's encoding surface, never by the vector's bytes: one text encoded in two batches differs in float noise.
  - Over the card and token trees, variant scripts left out.
  - It is checked on the geometry rather than the loss, because a collapsed space passes every average-case metric.

## Probes

- The knowledge probes are `scripts/effect_knowledge_probes/`, the embedding probes `scripts/effect_embedding_probes/`; their entry scripts are run by path.
- `common.probe_context(checkpoint)` refuses a checkpoint with no corpus, a corpus rebuilt since, or another input layout. `halves.load_selection` refuses a selection set frozen against another corpus or edited.
- The line and pair ladders read training records. They are reported only and never feed a stratum score.
- AUC and R² are never averaged together.
- The rung-3 `legal_target` readouts read the `target_legal` field, because the gate trains on no decision record.
- A line counts as held out only when every card carrying it is held out.
- The observed-mana label sums each `mana_produced` event's `mana_by_color`. An empty map is unreadable mana and counts for nothing.
- Every rung-3 head read is gated through `HeadReading`, except the ladder's `damage_taken`, read as the amount alone because every one of its items took damage.

## Selection set

- `selection-set-<corpus digest>.json`: canonical JSON with a `digest` over the rest, frozen once per curated corpus and found by the corpus digest, in the directory every reader is given (`--reports-dir` on `train-effect-model` and `evaluate-effect-model`, `--probe-set-dir` on the probes): the freeze's own `reports` run directory, passed to every reader of that corpus. `write_selection_set` writes it to a temporary name beside the path and moves it onto the path (`write_atomically`), so a kill leaves no truncated set (every later freeze would refuse it as another set) and a move refused by a file another process holds raises naming the path.
- Freeze it with `scripts/effect_knowledge_probes/run.py --freeze-probe-set --corpus DIR`; no checkpoint is needed. The freeze exits 1 when a half holds fewer than 300 games, a component's standard error passes 0.05 or a grid cell is empty.
- A grid is scored record by record: each cell holds the rule's outcome for each of the grid's records (`GridCell.answers`, in `FrozenGrid.records` order), and `score_scales.grid_score` compares each record's reading with that record's answer, its baseline the grid's mean answer against the same answers. Scored on cell means, a model reading each cell's base rate would score as one that knows the rule wherever the answer differs between records (a protection from mana value 3 or less). A set frozen before cells held `answers` is refused (`SelectionSetError`); freeze it again.
- The grid component weighs the damage, combat and evasion grids equally (`grid_edits.grid_of`): the combat grid's score is the mean of its variants', `combat/…` and `combat-several/…` alike, and a grid with no scored variant is left out. Averaged item by item, the combat variants would outweigh the other two grids many times over.
- Every grid cell is a board a record could show (`domain/grid_edits.py`); a cell no record shows scores the model on an input it never trained on.
  - A combat cell is a snapshot of its damage step, phase and substep both (`BoardEdit.set_step`, `board_edits.STEP_PHASES`).
  - A pair carrying a protection of any quality qualifies no combat grid (`grid_edits.changes_the_step`).
  - A double striker's second hit holds only boards where the first hit left the blocker alive (`grid_edits.Variant`): read at the blocker, the 28 cells whose attacker power is below the blocker's toughness (`held_cells`); read at the attacker, only the blocks whose own attacker power is below their blocker's toughness (`shows`). The freeze and the control score read a grid's cells from `CombatGrid.cells` and `GridEdits.cells`, which hold the same cells in the same order.
  - The damage grid qualifies a record only where the amount edit takes on both surfaces' texts, the line's script and its prose (`grid_edits.damage_target`): a prose stating no amount (a cost sentence alone) leaves every cell reading the real text against outcomes that vary by amount.
- The freeze reads the validation strata's shards the manifest lists, checked once against the disk before any record is read: an extra, missing or resized shard exits 1 naming it (`CorpusShardsError`), so a stale shard never reaches a frozen set. `evaluate-effect-model` checks at load and its reporting half and gates 1 and 2 read the context's listed paths. Every knowledge probe's record walk (`record_walk.records`/`qualifying`, method C's training stream) takes `ProbeContext.stratum_shards(stratum)` or `validation_shards(limit)`, the same checked listing, so a stale shard exits 1 naming it and no probe globs a curated directory; `--shards N` is the first N listed shards of each stratum.
- The control score scores what it cannot read at the floor: an item with any non-finite model reading (a probe or grid row, a rule-test record, a deviance term's loss) scores −1, never the mean of the rows it could read (`probe_item`, `rule_item`, `score_scales.grid_score`, `explained_deviance`). A label or answer that is not finite is unreadable data and leaves its row out as before. The epoch log's `non-finite readings:` line and the evaluation report's half detail give each stratum's count (`StratumScore.non_finite`).
- The control score's host cache (`application/host_cache.py`) rebuilds every tensor exactly, a float's negative zero with its sign bit: a zero is the bit pattern of zero (`_present`, `_differs`), so the packing pass costs a little more than a plain `!= 0`. `ControlScorer._values` scores inside `forward.running()`, so the modules stay in evaluation mode until the last read, whether or not the chunk generator `prepared` has been closed (a `zip` that ends on the chunk list leaves it open).
- A rule-test output whose mean target differs between the polarities by less than 1e-6 (`control_score.MIN_DIFFERENCE`) is not read: `_OutputReading.settle` refuses it, the rule's mean omits it and the stratum's `left_out` names it `rule/output`, or `rule` when none of the rule's outputs is left. The freeze does not test this (it holds no output's targets), so the selection set records no such output.
- A freeze never replaces a set already there by another: the same set again is a no-op, another exits 1 and leaves the file alone.
- Its halves (`selection`, `reporting`) are the manifest's and are never reassigned.
- The deviance sample is the one reader of a validation stratum that weighs its rows: each copy is weighed by its `curation.class_weight` (`rule_mask.weighed_by_class`) and every best constant is fitted at those weights (`loss_floor.constant_predictor_losses(weighted=True)`, `selection_deviance.TargetDevianceLosses`), so a row of class weight k scores as k repeated rows. Probes, grids, rule tests, the per-epoch validation loss and the gates take each record once and read no class weight; a second multiplication by it would square the class mix.
- A rule whose effective outputs are all per-entity (`rule_output_carriage.read_at_entities_only`, read in `RuleTestRules.entity_read`) is tested on the rows alone whose record carries one of the rule's effective outputs at a named outcome entity (`RuleTestRules.tests_of` reads `rule_output_carriage.carried_outputs`; `entity_read` maps each such rule to its outputs): only they count toward n and its standard error, and a rule with a polarity holding none is named in `left_out` as `no outcome entity` (`selection_set.NO_OUTCOME_ENTITY`). A row naming a player for a creature-zone output, say, reads nothing: the scorer counts it `untargeted`. Counting the other rows would freeze a test the control score can read on one polarity only, which then drops out of the mean unnoticed. A rule with a verdict, global or created-objects output keeps every row, read at the record.
- A rule test reads a per-entity field through the affected gate (`HeadReading`, the gate's probability times the field's), except on a record that trains no gate (`EntityTargetBatch.gate_scored` false: a cost half, a playability record, a rewrite whose outcome is partly unknown), where it reads the field head alone at the entities the targets mark affected (`control_score._ConditionalRead`), as training reads it. Through the gate, such a row reads a probability no target trained.
- A rule test's or a binary probe's standard error clusters by game (`score_scales.clustered_auc_standard_error`): `n₊` and `n₋` of the Hanley–McNeil bound are the distinct games among the item's positive rows and among its negative rows, so 40 records from 4 games count as 4 clusters, not 40 rows. Counting records would treat the several records of one game, which share a deck and a draw, as independent and understate the error. A grid's error counts the records it keeps. `stratum_placement` plans by the same count: a half's `n₊` and `n₋` for a rule are the games holding a rule-test-eligible record of each polarity, and its need (`rule_test_games_needed`, 68 at 0.1) is a number of games.
- A deviance item's standard error is `√(Σ_g S_g²) / Σ_g S_g` (`score_scales.loss_ratio_standard_error`), `S_g` summing over game g's copies the constant's loss times the copy's rule-mask weight times its class weight: `1/√n_eff`, with `n_eff = (Σ S_g)² / Σ S_g²` Kish's effective number of games.
- `train-effect-model` scores the selection halves each epoch and refuses a corpus with no set, or one frozen against another corpus or edited. `evaluate-effect-model` and the probes score the reporting halves.
