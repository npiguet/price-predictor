# Evaluation: gates, reported checks, probes and the selection set

Covers `evaluate-effect-model`, the three gates' thresholds, the knowledge and embedding probes, and the selection-set file. Read on demand: `src/effects/CLAUDE.md` says when, and lists the other topic files in this directory.

## `evaluate-effect-model`

- **`evaluate-effect-model`** — the three gates and the reported checks. **Splits are never a flag**: the held-out card list and both `game_id` sets come from `--checkpoint`, because the corpus is append-only and a recomputed split would score the gates partly on games the model trained on. A `--variant-checkpoint NAME=PATH` recording a different split or different vocabulary hashes fails fast. `--corpus` defaults to the dataset `--checkpoint` recorded; a checkpoint that records none is refused, and so is a corpus whose manifest digest has changed — a rebuilt dataset is a different split, so scoring the gates against it would score them partly on games the model trained on. `--variant-scripts` defaults to the tree that corpus's manifest recorded. The three FR-116 checks never built — matched real-vs-fork agreement, the probe-diff re-check and the role-polarity probe — report `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`, printed `[todo]` with what each would measure, on every run; they are not skips, because the corpus holds the fork, probe and mana records they would read. Every geometry check keys the cache on the checkpoint's encoding surface (`surface_of` its recorded vocabulary), so on a script checkpoint two lines sharing prose but not script stay two points. The ward canary's lines (`domain/ward_twins.py`) and the nearest-neighbour queries are `LineQuery`s — converted prose as `convert` spells it (`ward {2}`, `destroy target creature.`, `CARDNAME`), pinned to a card wherever that prose compiles to more than one script — resolved to the encoded key and printed back in prose; one that resolves to nothing is named in the report as unresolved, never dropped. The report is printed; the one check that draws a picture, UMAP (`application/umap_check.py`), writes `umap.png` and `umap.csv` into `<checkpoint stem>.evaluation/` beside `--checkpoint` and names both on its line. The picture has one panel per script API type, the twelve most frequent and the rest as `other`, each panel's texts over every other text: a dozen hues in one scatter cannot be told apart. The table holds each unique text's coordinates and its own API type, so a cluster seen in the picture can be looked up.

## The gates

| Gate | What it asks | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **1** — identity baseline | Is the encoder reading text? All three margins must hold on the card-disjoint split's unique-text stratum — resolution records whose acting text no record of the curated `training/` stratum acts with, which is the only slice where a baseline holding a free vector per text cannot simply recall the answer. The card-disjoint stratum is streamed as resolution records only. ≥ 0.05 absolute affected-gate F1 gain, ≥ 0.05 absolute zone-outcome accuracy gain (class-balanced over the observed outcomes, so answering "stayed" every time scores no better than chance), ≥ 5% relative negative-binomial deviance reduction, each model's deviance read under its own learned dispersions. A margin that cannot be measured fails. Three margins because they fail differently — the gate F1 says whether the model knows *that* something happens, the zone accuracy *what*, the deviance *how much*. | Pass/fail, reported; `evaluate-effect-model` exits 0 either way and prints `GATES FAILED: …` |
| **2** — damage-step keyword canary | Per keyword over the eight damage-step keywords: ≥ 200 qualifying records and ≥ 70% direction agreement under a **model-side** perturbation (the carrier stripped of the keyword: its kept keyword flag cleared and every line of the keyword dropped, printed or granted, a line a static grants included — `SurfaceBuilder.build`'s strip). The population is the real combats of the game-disjoint games — `validation/game-disjoint` of the curated corpus — and a probe fork or an intervention qualifies nothing. The 200 counts distinct `record_id`s; agreement averages over (record, carrier) observations, each carrier being its own perturbation. Fields are read through `HeadReading`: the gate's probability times the expected value for counts and deltas, times the class probability for categoricals, since a field head trains only where an entity was affected. Under-sampled routes the same way as wrong — treating untested as passed is how a canary stops being one. | Routing only — its per-keyword verdict is what decides whether the probe machinery is worth building at all |
| **3** — collapse canaries | Mean pairwise cosine ≤ 0.5 over 10,000 random pairs, top principal component ≤ 30% of variance, over one vector per unique ability text — unique by the text on the checkpoint's encoding surface, never by the vector's bytes (one text encoded in two batches differs in float noise), over the card and token trees with variant scripts left out. Checked on the geometry rather than the loss, because a collapsed space passes every average-case metric. | Pass/fail, reported, as gate 1 |

## Reported checks and probes

**Evaluation.** `evaluate-effect-model` adds per-text card-disjoint results by rarity bucket and family (and averaged over families), the per-family memorization gap, a random-seat slice, a real-decision legality slice, and the withheld keyword's zero-shot slice (`application/breakdowns.py`), each over distinct records (a validation stratum repeats a record's row for every added copy; gates 1 and 2 read each record once too); then the **reporting half** (FR-070): the selection set frozen for the corpus (found by its digest under `output/effects/reports/`, the config's `reports_dir`, no flag; reported as skipped without one) scored once through the control score's scorer (`control_score.score_half`, a `CheckpointPreparer` over the checkpoint's own batcher, its recorded `--rule-mask`, `--stratum-weight` and `--deviance-floor`, lenient: a component with nothing to score is NaN and named), per stratum its four components and score, each component's items, the rule tests per rule (`rule-tests <stratum> reporting`), and the control score beside the selection half's best the checkpoint recorded. It exits 1 on a corpus not wholly gen-3 or a checkpoint of another input layout, and 0 after any gate verdict; `--win-rates` names the decodability battery's file. The embedding probes (`scripts/effect_embedding_probes/`) take `--checkpoint` and `--abilities-root`; `effect_profiles.py` counts each curated record once, merging the training stratum's `--reuse-cap` copies by `record_id` across shards. The knowledge probes (`scripts/effect_knowledge_probes/`) are an importable package — `board_edits` (the package's `effects.domain.board_edits`, the record edits every grid and sweep makes; the grids' own edits are `effects.domain.grid_edits`, which the control score edits through too), `record_walk` (the per-predicate, per-game-capped record reader, gen-3 shards only and each record once unless told otherwise), `halves` (a selection-set half read once) and `scorecard` beside the original modules — whose entry scripts are still run by path. `common.probe_context(checkpoint)` resolves the curated corpus, the sidecar roots and the variant tree from the checkpoint and its manifest, refusing a checkpoint with no corpus, a corpus rebuilt since, or a checkpoint of another input layout than `gen-3`, and `halves.load_selection` refuses a selection set frozen against another corpus or edited. `run.py --freeze-probe-set --corpus DIR` freezes the corpus's selection set with no checkpoint (`application/freeze_selection_set.py`, `domain/selection_set.py`, written to `output/effects/reports/selection-set-<corpus digest>.json`): per validation stratum and half, read from the manifest's halves and never reassigned, the rung-3 probe items, the damage, combat and evasion edit grids' records (one a game, then the fewest by smallest keyed hash that bring a grid's worst-case standard error to 0.09, at most 512, both recorded in the set's settings; T160), the rule tests (the detectors' tags on rule-test-eligible records of trained rules without an unpatched confirmed departure, each frozen with its outcome entities, each rule's polarities balanced to r = 4 by a seeded draw; a rule with no effective output named untested in every half) and the deviance sample (every copy, at most 16,384 a half), each item's worst-case standard error (a deviance item's — any shipped head's output term, `selection_deviance.TargetDevianceLosses` — under both rule masks), and every item left out above 0.1 or untested; it exits 1 naming stratum, half and component when a half holds fewer than 300 games, a component's standard error passes 0.05 or a grid cell is empty. The probe scripts hand it their labels and grids through ports (`scripts/effect_knowledge_probes/selection.py`), and `combat_grid.py` and `evasion_grid.py` read the records it froze (`--half`, reporting by default) rather than scanning shards, as `damage_grid.py` and `ladder_check.py` do. A probing run (`run.py --checkpoint`) reads the halves of the selection set frozen for its checkpoint's corpus, each half's games once and each record once (a validation stratum repeats a record's row for every added copy), and writes one scorecard: per stratum and half, the ladder over the frozen probe items (each probe type's rung-3 score beside it on the control score's scale), the frozen grids scored as the control score scores them (`1 − MAE / B`), the rule tests (`halves.score_rule_tests`, through the control score's own scorer, `control_score.score_half`: per rule, the mean over its effective outputs, read at the tag's outcome entities and only where the record's targets define the output, of `2·AUC − 1` ranking `fires` above `does_not_fire`, the category and direction read off the records' own targets), and the turn and step conditions, anthem and target-legality probes; the sweeps over the reporting half. The line and pair ladders read training records, each once, are reported only and never feed a stratum score. `compare.py` sets scorecards side by side and ranks per probe type and per score (AUC and R² never averaged together), and per stratum and half on the probe types' AUC and R² apart, the grids and the rule tests. The rung-3 `legal_target` readouts read the `target_legal` field, which gen-3 trains on every card and player of a targeting decision (the gate trains on no decision record). A line counts as held out only when every card carrying it is held out. The observed-mana label sums each `mana_produced` event's `mana_by_color`; an empty map is unreadable mana and counts for nothing, and a record with nothing else gets no label. Every rung-3 head read is gated through `HeadReading` except the ladder's `damage_taken`, read as the amount alone, because every one of its items took damage.

## Selection set

**Selection set** (`output/effects/reports/selection-set-<corpus digest>.json`, written by `scripts/effect_knowledge_probes/run.py --freeze-probe-set --corpus DIR`): canonical JSON, `digest` a sha256 over the rest, frozen once per curated corpus and found by its digest. Per validation stratum and half (`selection` | `reporting`, the manifest's `halves`, never reassigned): the probe items (`record_id`, `game_id`, entity, label), the edit grids (cells with the rule's outcome, records), the rule tests (`fires` and `does_not_fire` records with their tag entities, balanced to `r` = 4), the deviance sample (`record_id`, `game_id`, `copy`); plus `probe_kinds`, `standard_errors` (`stratum/half/component/item`), `left_out` (item and reason, `untested` included) and `settings` (the thresholds it ran under). `train-effect-model` scores the selection halves each epoch and refuses a corpus with no set, or one frozen against another corpus or edited; `evaluate-effect-model` and the probes score the reporting halves. Contract: `specs/025-ability-effect-model-gen3/contracts/selection-set.md`.
