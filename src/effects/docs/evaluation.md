# Evaluation: gates, reported checks, probes and the selection set

Covers `evaluate-effect-model`, the three gates' thresholds, the knowledge and embedding probes, and the selection-set file. Read on demand: `src/effects/CLAUDE.md` says when, and lists the other topic files in this directory.

## `evaluate-effect-model`

- **`evaluate-effect-model`** — the three gates and the reported checks. **Splits are never a flag**: the held-out card list and both `game_id` sets come from `--checkpoint`, because the corpus is append-only and a recomputed split would score the gates partly on games the model trained on. A `--variant-checkpoint NAME=PATH` recording a different split or different vocabulary hashes fails fast. `--corpus` defaults to the dataset `--checkpoint` recorded; a checkpoint that records none is refused, and so is a corpus whose manifest digest has changed — a rebuilt dataset is a different split, so scoring the gates against it would score them partly on games the model trained on. The three FR-116 checks never built — matched real-vs-fork agreement, the probe-diff re-check and the role-polarity probe — report `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`, printed `[todo]` with what each would measure, on every run; they are not skips, because the corpus holds the fork, probe and mana records they would read. Every geometry check keys the cache on the checkpoint's encoding surface (`surface_of` its recorded vocabulary), so on a script checkpoint two lines sharing prose but not script stay two points.

## The gates

| Gate | What it asks | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **1** — identity baseline | Is the encoder reading text? All three margins must hold on the card-disjoint split's unique-text stratum — resolution records whose acting text no record of the curated `training/` stratum acts with, which is the only slice where a baseline holding a free vector per text cannot simply recall the answer. The card-disjoint stratum is streamed as resolution records only. ≥ 0.05 absolute affected-gate F1 gain, ≥ 0.05 absolute zone-outcome accuracy gain (class-balanced over the observed outcomes, so answering "stayed" every time scores no better than chance), ≥ 5% relative negative-binomial deviance reduction, each model's deviance read under its own learned dispersions. A margin that cannot be measured fails. Three margins because they fail differently — the gate F1 says whether the model knows *that* something happens, the zone accuracy *what*, the deviance *how much*. | Pass/fail, reported; `evaluate-effect-model` exits 0 either way and prints `GATES FAILED: …` |
| **2** — damage-step keyword canary | Per keyword over the eight damage-step keywords: ≥ 200 qualifying records and ≥ 70% direction agreement under a **model-side** perturbation (the carrier stripped of the keyword: its kept keyword flag cleared and every line of the keyword dropped, printed or granted, a line a static grants included — `SurfaceBuilder.build`'s strip). The population is the real combats of the game-disjoint games — `validation/game-disjoint` of the curated corpus — and a probe fork or an intervention qualifies nothing. The 200 counts distinct `record_id`s; agreement averages over (record, carrier) observations, each carrier being its own perturbation. Fields are read through `HeadReading`: the gate's probability times the expected value for counts and deltas, times the class probability for categoricals, since a field head trains only where an entity was affected. Under-sampled routes the same way as wrong — treating untested as passed is how a canary stops being one. | Routing only — its per-keyword verdict is what decides whether the probe machinery is worth building at all |
| **3** — collapse canaries | Mean pairwise cosine ≤ 0.5 over 10,000 random pairs, top principal component ≤ 30% of variance, over one vector per unique ability text — unique by the text on the checkpoint's encoding surface, never by the vector's bytes (one text encoded in two batches differs in float noise), over the card and token trees with variant scripts left out. Checked on the geometry rather than the loss, because a collapsed space passes every average-case metric. | Pass/fail, reported, as gate 1 |

## Reported checks and probes

Beyond the gates, `evaluate-effect-model` prints breakdowns by rarity bucket and family, the random-seat, real-decision and withheld-keyword slices (`application/breakdowns.py`), and the selection set's reporting half scored through the control score's scorer; each reads a record once, though a validation stratum repeats a record's row for every added copy. It exits 1 on a corpus not wholly gen-3 or a checkpoint of another input layout, and 0 after any gate verdict; `--win-rates` names the decodability battery's file. `common.probe_context(checkpoint)` resolves the curated corpus, the sidecar roots and the variant tree from the checkpoint and its manifest, refusing a checkpoint with no corpus, a corpus rebuilt since, or a checkpoint of another input layout than `gen-3`, and `halves.load_selection` refuses a selection set frozen against another corpus or edited. The line and pair ladders read training records, each once, are reported only and never feed a stratum score. The rung-3 `legal_target` readouts read the `target_legal` field, which gen-3 trains on every card and player of a targeting decision (the gate trains on no decision record). A line counts as held out only when every card carrying it is held out. The observed-mana label sums each `mana_produced` event's `mana_by_color`; an empty map is unreadable mana and counts for nothing, and a record with nothing else gets no label. Every rung-3 head read is gated through `HeadReading` except the ladder's `damage_taken`, read as the amount alone, because every one of its items took damage.

## Selection set

**Selection set** (`output/effects/reports/selection-set-<corpus digest>.json`, written by `scripts/effect_knowledge_probes/run.py --freeze-probe-set --corpus DIR`): canonical JSON, `digest` a sha256 over the rest, frozen once per curated corpus and found by its digest. `train-effect-model` scores the selection halves each epoch and refuses a corpus with no set, or one frozen against another corpus or edited; `evaluate-effect-model` and the probes score the reporting halves. Contract: `specs/025-ability-effect-model-gen3/contracts/selection-set.md`.
