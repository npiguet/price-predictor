# Training

Covers `train-effect-model`, its auxiliary terms and flags, and how every loss is built. Read on demand: `src/effects/CLAUDE.md` says when, and lists the other topic files in this directory.

## `train-effect-model`

- **`train-effect-model`** — trains both transformers jointly from random init. `--corpus` is required and is the trainer's only input (FR-125 withdrawn): a curated dataset built by `build-corpus` carries the split, both validation strata and the rarity table, so the trainer neither computes a holdout nor sweeps the raw corpus at startup — everything it reads comes from the manifest or from `validation/samples/`, and `--variant-scripts` defaults to the tree the manifest records, as evaluation's does. The split holds out **ability texts**, not cards, decided once by `build-corpus`: a text at most `--holdout-max-carriers` cards carry is held out when `crc32` of its normalized script text modulo 1000 falls below `--holdout-permille`, and a card carrying one is a held-out card. Keyed on the text because the model never reads a card's name and functional reprints compile to the same script — a name-keyed holdout trains on Lightning Strike while holding out Searing Spear. Membership depends on the text's own bytes, so a Forge upgrade never reassigns an existing text, which is what lets a depleted corpus stay valid. Every game holding a record that names a held-out card was already excluded from the training stratum when the dataset was built — records from one game share a board, so a held-out card in the context of a training record would otherwise leak through its context role even when it is not the acting ability — so the trainer never filters a shard it reads: it refuses one holding a game the manifest places in a validation stratum (`ValidationGameInTrainingError`, exit 1), since that shard and the manifest come from different builds. The training corpus is read **one shard at a time** (`--shards-per-epoch`), never whole, and an epoch's shards are **drawn at random across the whole training-shard list** rather than taken as a contiguous run of it — the list is in path order, so a block is one collection run's consecutive worker lifetimes and never leaves the family that sorts first. The optimizer holds **two** parameter groups, `encoder` and `head` (plus `identity` for that variant), so `clip_per_group` reports — and clips — the encoder apart from the head: whether the encoder's gradient is reaching it at all through the `e` path is exactly what its own number now has to show, rather than one norm the head's larger gradient could dominate. Within the resident shard, records weight ∝ `effective_games^(−0.5)`, normalized within each sampling class and capped at 20× the weight of the text at the 99th percentile of effective games — **games, not records**, so one long game producing hundreds of records of one ability does not make it look well-observed — and that weight drives a **weighted shuffle without replacement** over the whole shard rather than a per-batch class re-draw: one shuffle cuts the shard into full `--batch-size` batches, dropping the ragged tail and beginning a fresh shuffle, instead of resampling the shard with replacement batch by batch. The eight-class sampling mixture is fixed once, on disk, by `build-corpus --class-mix`; the trainer samples what the corpus holds rather than re-mixing per batch, and fields whose record kinds are absent from the corpus contribute no loss. Per-epoch validation is scored in `--batch-size` batches over the shuffled sample, never one game per batch: `active_fields` keeps a field only while some class in the batch supervises it, so a whole-game batch measures a smaller objective than the training batch printed beside it, and a stratum's loss becomes a mean over as many numbers as it has games. Each epoch line also carries gate 1's three margins over the whole card-disjoint sample, read off that validation pass, so they are a progress reading rather than the evaluator's gate, which scores the unique-text stratum alone. Validation, gate 1, the breakdowns, the knowledge probes and the control score read the model through one chunked forward (`application/record_forward.py`): evaluation mode, no gradient, and bf16 autocast on CUDA for all of them, so a checkpoint is reported at the precision it was selected at. Validation, early stopping and every saved checkpoint read the **averaged weights** (`application/weight_average.py`), an exponential moving average started at warmup's end, never the trained ones. The run keeps one best checkpoint, encoder, trunk and heads together, from the epoch with the best **control score** on the averaged weights (FR-069; `training_schedule.ControlScoreStopper`, higher is better): `--patience` counts epochs without a new best, a new best copies `resume.pt` to `resume-best.pt`, and the curriculum switch resets the best and the patience. The control score (`application/control_score.py`) reads the selection set frozen for the corpus, found by the corpus digest under `--reports-dir` (default `output/effects/reports/`); without one `train-effect-model` exits 1 at startup (`SelectionSetError`). The card-disjoint validation loss stays reported, with its per-field breakdown; each field there carries its explained deviance against a constant-predictor floor — the loss the best constant prediction for that field reaches on the same sample, logged on its own line and recomputed only when the curriculum changes the active field set — because a loss in nats alone cannot say whether the head learned the field or learned how often it fires. `--variant {full,identity,state-only,no-state,taxonomy}` trains the four evaluation baselines on the identical pipeline; every variant run inherits its split from `--corpus`'s manifest, which is the only place a split comes from — the trainer has no `--split-from`, `--context-cache` or `--cache-refresh`, and context abilities are always re-encoded live. `--withhold-keyword` holds one implemented keyword's token out of training so the zero-shot check has something to measure. One tokenizer path does it, `AbilityTokenizer.encoding_tokens`: it force-expands the withheld keyword to its reminder text wherever it appears, then drops the keyword's token everywhere it is left, inside another keyword's expansion included (flying's reminder text names flying). Training, evaluation and `encode-abilities` all encode through it, the last reading the keyword from the checkpoint's provenance, so the model reads what the keyword does and never the token itself, and the cache matches. `--grad-accum` counts its micro-steps within the epoch, so a partial accumulation left at an epoch's end is dropped rather than shifting every later epoch's optimizer boundary. On CUDA the forward pass and loss run under bf16 autocast, the per-process CUDA memory reservation is capped, and the allocator's cache is released once per shard — the caching allocator otherwise fragments on the corpus's variable batch shapes and reserves far more than it ever allocates, which a Windows driver pages to host RAM instead of raising an out-of-memory error.

## Training

Noise on `e` is applied in the batcher, in training only, as Gaussian noise with covariance `r²Σ`. A training-only value head reads `e` alone and predicts script-stated amounts and cost flags (`domain/value_targets.py`; spell lines mask mana costs) at `--value-weight` (0.05). Its targets are read from the line's sidecar `script_text` on both surfaces, since prose has no `$` parameter to read, and so are the script-API head's. `MoveCounter`, `AddOrRemoveCounter` and `ReplaceCounter` mask both counter targets, and any non-literal value masks its target; beside each amount it predicts whether the magnitude is at least k for k = 1…40 (`AT_LEAST_SLICES`, averaged so the block weighs one binary target), which pushes `e` to carry amounts as a thermometer the trunk can compare. The MLM pass zeroes a masked position's numeric value and its number flag along with the token, since the encoder adds the number embedding beside the token's and would otherwise hand a masked digit back. `--api-weight` defaults to 0.003, which starts the script-API term beside the other auxiliaries rather than above them. **Input layout** (`effect_head_input.py`): a checkpoint records `training_settings["input_layout"]`, and `effect_model_store` refuses on load (`CheckpointLayoutError`, exit 1) any checkpoint recording another layout or none, so every loader reads only the layout this code builds; earlier layouts no longer load. The layout is `gen-3` (`model-layout.md`), and `layout_settings()` records beside it the relation types, the subtypes and named counters and the closed output lists, whose orders fix table rows and columns, so a checkpoint whose lists differ is refused the same way. Gen-2 maps Forge's `combat_*` phase names, `+1/+1`/`-1/-1` counters and `protection_from_<x>` onto the vocabularies' slots (they used to land in "other"), appends a `landwalk` keyword slot, and ends each card slot with thermometer codes of total power, total toughness and toughness minus marked damage and each player slot with one of life (`THERMOMETER_STEPS = 40`, the magnitude cap, since life often passes 20), so a stated amount compares with them in one attention score. Every count and signed-delta magnitude is scored at no more than 40 (`MAGNITUDE_CAP`), in the field losses, the created-objects counts, the constant-predictor floor and the evaluation's deviance: above that an amount is the game compounding, not the text, and one Bristly Bill game's +2,098,760 power spiked a shard's loss to 169k. `--lethality-copies K` (`domain/lethality_counterfactuals.py`, FR-063e) appends to each training step K copies of every damage or -X/-X resolution whose chain cannot turn on the death and whose real outcome obeys rule 704.5f-g: one hit creature's toughness and the amount (in the text under a `counterfactual/<amount>/…` key the step's batcher reads as an override, `SurfaceBatcher.override_text` — the script's amount on the script surface, the converted prose's amount on the prose surface with its role spans shifted by the edit — or in `refs.x` for a paid X) are redrawn from 1 to 8 and the deaths relabelled by the rule. Forge's AI aims removal at creatures it can kill, so without them the corpus rarely shows damage falling short and the model learns "small creatures die" instead of comparing the amount with the toughness. K = 0 trains bit for bit as before.

## Losses

Every head reduces one way (`effect_losses.per_row`): a row's loss is summed over its targets, then averaged over the batch's rows, a row being a record for the per-entity, created-objects and verdict heads and an encoded text for the value, script-API and MLM heads; the value head's forty at-least bits are still averaged into one binary target's weight. On the per-entity head the affected gate trains on every entity of a record that is not a playability record (`EntityTargetBatch.gated`, root spec § 11.4: a decision or declaration observes which entities could be chosen, not what changed), and a field trains only where the gate's target fires **and** the field is present (`EntityFieldTargets` in `entity_field_targets.py`); the legality fields (`FieldSpec.gated` false: `target_legal`, `attacker_legal`, `blocker_legal`, `min_blockers`, `attack_target_legal`, `combat_requirement`) train wherever present, gate or no gate, `target_legal` on every card and player of a decision whose candidate has a targeting clause (1 in `legal_targets`, 0 elsewhere). A field is present where the record wrote it, or where it has a silent value, the record's sampling class supervises it and the entity is of the field's `FieldScope` (a player is never scored on a zone, a permanent never on life). Silent values: zone = stayed; damage, counters and P/T change = 0; types, colours and keywords gained or lost = none; control and attached-to change = 0; a player's life, cards drawn/discarded/milled, library events, poison and mana = 0. `tap_state`, both durations, the legality bits and `min_blockers` have none and train only where an event or a payload sets them, because a creature no event mentions is neither tapped nor untapped. `continuous` records supervise the P/T, keyword, type and colour fields too, and a `=` in a contribution's `types` or `colors` marks the matching `*_lost` field unknown, which no silent value overrides. A `trigger` record derives no per-entity targets — its events are what caused it, and what it then does arrives as its resolution record — so it supervises the verdict head's fired bit, and the gate trains on its entities as negatives. Per-entity count fields are scored from 0 to the cap, never below zero (`damage_taken` nets healing). Every shipped term — the gate and each per-entity field, each verdict and global field, each created-objects slot column — is divided by its loss denominator (FR-062): its constant-prediction loss in nats per record (`loss_floor.constant_predictor_losses`) over eight training shards drawn under `--seed` before the first step (`application/denominator_pass.py`), clamped below at `--deviance-floor` (0.04), and a term the sample never supervised divides by the floor. The denominators go into `resume.pt` and every checkpoint (`loss_denominators`), and a resume reuses them rather than recomputing. The loss training descends and validation's loss are both scaled; the parts the shard and epoch lines print stay in nats, so they still compare with the constant-predictor floor. **The rule mask** (`--rule-mask`, FR-061, `domain/rule_mask.py`) weighs each curated copy's outputs from its `curation` and each rule's effective outputs (`CorpusFacts.rule_outputs`, the manifest's `effective_rule_outputs`): under `hard` (default) a copy rule budgets added trains only the effective outputs of every rule its record was drawn for — per-entity fields wherever the record supervises them, verdict and global fields, `created_objects` as one head — and the affected gate on its drawn rule's tag entities alone, condition and outcome alike (`Curation.rule_entities`), so a copy whose rule names no entity in either role trains no gate and one whose rule names an output the record lacks trains the gate alone; under `soft` every copy trains every output, the drawn rules' at weight 1 and the rest at `natural_share / copies`. A natural copy of a record with no added copy, a natural copy of an aimed record and a lethality copy train everything at full weight; the value, script-API and MLM heads read texts and no mask reaches them. Evaluation (`per_entity_targets`) reads records unmasked.
